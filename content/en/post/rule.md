This is a blog rule.

To maintain a polite, friendly, and tidy communication environment, and create a pleasant space for reading and discussion, all visitors and commentators must abide by the following rules.
1. Basic Rules
Speak politely and discuss rationally. Respect the owner and every user. Personal attacks, sarcasm, and provocation are prohibited.
Use civilized language. No vulgar words, insults, inappropriate content, discrimination, or offensive remarks.
Discuss content reasonably. You may ask questions, share opinions, give feedback, or leave a message. Spam and repetitive comments are not allowed.
Follow internet laws. Do not post illegal, violent, sensitive, or copyrighted content.
2. Prohibited Behavior
No quarreling, trolling, targeting others, or creating conflicts.
No advertising, external links, promotion, QR codes, or commercial content.
Do not plagiarize, repost without permission, or leak others’ private information.
Do not flood the comment area with meaningless sentences, repeated emojis, or useless messages.
No rumors, false information, or malicious defamation.
3. Comment Etiquette
Ask questions clearly and politely to ensure timely replies. Friendly suggestions and corrections are welcome.
Share your thoughts freely and respect different viewpoints.
Avoid messy symbols, garbled text, or long meaningless paragraphs. Keep the comment area clean.
Communicate in an orderly manner during group discussions.
4. Violation Handling
Minor violation: irrelevant content or impolite words — warning and comment deletion.
Moderate violation: trolling, vulgar content, advertising — comment cleaning and temporary restriction.
Severe violation: abuse, illegal content, continuous disturbance — permanent block and comment ban.
5. Additional Notice
All comments represent only the personal opinions of visitors, not the position of this blog.
The blog owner reserves the right to review, delete, manage, and block comments.
These rules will be adjusted appropriately in the future.
Thank you for your kindness and cooperation. Wish you a pleasant time here.
